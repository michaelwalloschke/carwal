## What Went Well

- Michael: epic felt very positive, nothing stood out as a pain point.
- Solid AD-1 foundation (six context roots from Story 1.1) — no friction in any later story.
- Adversarial review process (Blind Hunter / Edge Case Hunter / Acceptance Auditor) consistently caught reachable bugs a spec-only pass would have missed: malformed magic-link token `MatchError` and no-JS POST `FunctionClauseError` (1.2), `CARWAL_DOMAIN` injection risk (1.3).
- Review rounds converged over the epic: 1.1 took 4 passes (including a self-inflicted regression from its own patch), 1.3 took 4 (incl. one CodeRabbit-triggered pass), but 1.4 and 1.5 each closed in a single consolidated pass — later stories visibly benefited from patterns established earlier.
- Reusable patterns established and never re-invented: the `read_env` empty-string-safe env parsing helper (born in 1.2, reused in 1.3/1.4), the raise-on-missing-required-prod-env contract (SECRET_KEY_BASE-style, applied consistently to SMTP creds, PHX_HOST/PORT, VAPID keys), and PII-out-of-git config via runtime env injection.
- Deferred-work discipline: nearly every code-review-deferred item was tagged with an explicit target story or "ongoing maintenance" in `deferred-work.md`, keeping technical debt visible and assigned rather than silently dropped.

## Challenges / Patterns to Watch

- **gettext/i18n compliance slipped past the first implementation pass** in Stories 1.1 (hardcoded German strings, multiple patch rounds to route through gettext) and 1.2 (3 flashes stayed English until the two-axis review caught them). Root cause: not verified during implementation, only in review.
- **Env-var edge cases (empty string, negative, out-of-range) recurred across two stories** before the `read_env` pattern stabilized — same bug class caught three separate times (1.1 → 1.3 `PORT=""` → 1.3 Pass 3 `Integer.parse` accepting out-of-range values).
- **Local dev-machine collision:** PostgreSQL port 5432 was already bound by another project's container; rebound to 5433 in Story 1.1 and the convention carried forward cleanly through the rest of the epic.
- **Process gap in the defer mechanism itself:** Story 1.5's review found a Dev-Notes-level "deferred" call-out (SSH hardening) that hadn't been mirrored into `deferred-work.md` — a small but real leak in an otherwise-reliable discipline.
- **Test assertions coupling to transient copy:** Story 1.1's `page_controller_test` pinned a literal English promo string and broke the moment that copy changed; loosened to a stable brand substring match.

## Technical Debt Carried Into Epic 2

- ~30 open items in `deferred-work.md`, almost all Low/Nit priority; none block Epic 2's ingestion work — infra is clean for the ingestion spike.
- One Medium item: `restore.sh`'s remote-host scratch-mode path has never been exercised against a real second host (only local-scratch was validated during the 1.5 rehearsal). Re-verify when a second host becomes available.
- Minor: `env_file` in `deploy/compose.yml` leaks app secrets/PII into the Postgres container's env — low risk under the household threat model, worth narrowing on a future hardening pass.
